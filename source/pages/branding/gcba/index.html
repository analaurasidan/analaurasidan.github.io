GCBA.Public information, made approachable.

Banners, social media guidance and awareness content for consumer protection in Buenos Aires City.

Client / context
GCBA · Consumer Protection

01 / Print communication

Consumer rights, beyond the screen.

Large-format banners introduce consumer rights and the BA Concilia service. Short messages, strong typographic hierarchy and clear access points help structure information for a physical setting.

02 / Social media

Turning a process into clear steps.

The service cancellation carousel breaks a complaint process into a sequence of actions. Numbered steps, illustration and contrasting text panels help guide the reader from the problem to the next step.

03 / Awareness campaign

Small habits. A shared message.

The Earth Day series connects everyday actions with environmental awareness. A nature-inspired palette and illustration give each topic its own focus while keeping the four pieces visually connected.

04 / Selected reel frames

Information with a visual rhythm.

Two visual approaches for social content: a colourful sequence explaining how to check a property administrator’s registration, and a commemorative piece for May 25 using collage, national colours and typography.
