01 / Social media system

Making healthcare easier to navigate.

The social media redesign brings health advice, service information and promotional content into a clearer visual language. Recognisable colours, typographic hierarchy and flexible layouts keep the brand consistent while giving each message its own emphasis.

A shared visual language, adapted to different kinds of information. Select an image to view it in full.

02 / Referral campaign

Friend to Friend.

A referral campaign built around friendship and shared wellbeing. Speech bubbles, warm photography and blue-to-green gradients connect an emotional message with a clear invitation to recommend the health plan.

03 / Performance campaign

What's Your Plan? — Phase 2

The second phase of the campaign moves towards more direct, action-oriented communication. A shared visual frame, blue-to-green gradients and a prominent call to action connect the display, horizontal, feed and story formats, adapting each message to a different healthcare need.

One campaign system across five display, horizontal, feed and story adaptations. Select the composition to view it in full.

04 / Business communication

A different audience. The same visual clarity.

For business audiences, the focus shifts to services, workplace wellbeing and product information. Blue-and-orange layouts, clear feature lists and product-led compositions connect social campaigns with LinkedIn content.
