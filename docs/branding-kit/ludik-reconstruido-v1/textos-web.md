## 01 — Emergencias Salud

**Título de tarjeta:** Emergencias Salud

**Categoría:** Visual design · Social media · Campaigns

**Descripción de tarjeta:** A refreshed social media system and campaign visuals that bring clarity and consistency to healthcare communication.

### Overview

A social media redesign for Emergencias Salud, developed through HG Agency. The project focused on creating a clearer, more contemporary visual presence and organising different types of healthcare content within a recognisable system.

### Design approach

Content categories, typography and a blue-and-green visual language help connect service information, health advice and promotional messages. The system extends across feed posts, carousels and stories, with layouts adapted to each format.

### Selected campaigns

Friend to Friend explores referral messages through friendship and shared wellbeing. What's Your Plan? — Phase 2 applies a more direct, action-oriented approach to communicating access to medical care. Together, they show how the visual system adapts to different campaign goals.

**Piezas para mostrar:** rediseño general, selección de carruseles y stories, Friend to Friend y What's Your Plan? No hace falta incluir todas las variantes.

## 02 — Incode Argentina · Zebra

**Título de tarjeta:** Incode Argentina · Zebra

**Categoría:** Digital communication · Email design · Social media

**Descripción de tarjeta:** Email and social media design that translates technology products into clear, consistent visual communication.

### Overview

A selection of newsletters and social media content for Incode Argentina, including communication featuring Zebra products. The work brings product information, imagery and benefits together within an established brand language.

### Design approach

Clear information hierarchy helps readers identify the product and understand its main features. Layouts balance technical content with product photography, while colour and typography keep the communication consistent across email and social formats.

**Piezas para mostrar:** una newsletter completa, un detalle legible y dos o tres publicaciones complementarias. Identificar Incode como contexto del trabajo y Zebra como marca de los productos, según corresponda; no presentar una contratación directa de Zebra sin confirmación.

## 03 — GCBA · Consumer Protection

**Título de tarjeta:** GCBA · Consumer Protection

**Categoría:** Public communication · Social media · Campaigns

**Descripción de tarjeta:** Visual content that makes public information and consumer guidance easier to understand and explore.

### Overview

Social media content for the consumer protection area of the Buenos Aires City Government. The selection covers practical guidance, environmental awareness and a commemorative piece for Argentina's May Revolution.

### Design approach

Each topic calls for a different visual treatment while keeping the message easy to follow. Step-by-step carousels organise guidance on service cancellation; Earth Day content uses illustration and a nature-inspired palette; the May 25 piece combines collage and national colours.

**Piezas para mostrar:** carrusel de baja de servicio, Día de la Tierra y pieza del 25 de Mayo. Para la última, usar video solo si se dispone del archivo; una imagen fija no demuestra la animación.

## 04 — Ludik Agency

**Título de tarjeta:** Ludik Agency

**Categoría:** Brand identity · Print design · Merchandising

**Descripción de tarjeta:** A playful visual identity that connects bold typography, collage and colour across posters, stationery and merchandise.

### Overview

Ludik explores creativity through play and movement. Its visual identity brings that idea into a flexible language of bold typography, geometric forms and expressive collage.

### Design approach

A deep navy base creates contrast with coral, peach and turquoise accents. The poster series combines photographic fragments, rings and oversized lettering, while stationery and merchandise extend the identity into physical applications.

**Piezas para mostrar:** afiches, selección de papelería y una aplicación de merchandising ya diseñada. Diferenciar las piezas originales de cualquier nueva propuesta de mockup.
